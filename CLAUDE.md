# ps-1-rpg: notes for Claude

PS1-style action RPG in Godot 4.7 (GDScript). Joseph works on it from two
computers (laptop and desktop); this file carries the state between them.
Also read `docs/COWORK_HANDOFF.md` (cloud/local session log) before editing
the street areas or the shop.

## How to work here
- Tools: the Beckett MCP runs and inspects the Godot editor/game (Lite: it can
  see the game but can't send input, so test with temporary driver scenes in
  `scenes/_claude_test/` and delete them after). Headless Godot for tool
  scripts: `godot --headless --path . -s res://scripts/tools/<tool>.gd`.
  The Godot exe path differs per machine (laptop:
  `C:\Users\sosas\OneDrive\Desktop\Godot_v4.7-stable_win64.exe`).
- Tuning lives in Resources (`.tres`, edited in the Inspector), not in code.
- Joseph prefers plain-language summaries. Don't commit unless he asks.

## Machine gotcha: per-machine import cache
`.godot/` is per machine. If the player shows up in a T-pose or a model looks
wrong on one computer only, its import is stale: reimport the model (select it
in the FileSystem dock > Import > Reimport). The player model GLB relies on its
post-import script (`scripts/tools/newmain_base_import.gd`).

## DON'T re-run the street level generator
The five areas `scenes/areas/area_{terrace_street,shop_corner,estate,underpass,downtown}.tscn`
were generated by `scripts/tools/street_kit/gen_levels.py`, then **edited
directly** in later sessions. Re-running the generator wipes all of this:
- daytime: `city_day_environment.tres` + a Sun (no rain, no moon), and the
  street lamps' light/cone removed
- boundary buildings replaced by `CityBuilding`s (node `Buildings`), set back
  3 m with a second sidewalk row and trees in grass pits
- the alleys rebuilt from CityBuildings plus props, lots and gaps filled
  (node `Nooks`)
- the Terrace Street alley loot sack fix (`s_alley_potions`)
Port changes into the generator first if it ever has to run again.

## Systems and where they live

**Player model:** `scenes/player_model_newmain.tscn` (Tripo GLB
`Import/Character/MC-Rig/NewMain_Base.glb`). The old `player_model.tscn` is kept
as a backup. Its animations (`resources/player/player_animations_newmain.tres`)
are generated from the old library by
`scripts/tools/retarget_player_animations.gd`. That tool also bakes the
"organic" pass and the fashion styling (line-up idle, runway walk/run): tune
the constants at its top and rerun it. Old clip tools (`build_*_clip.gd`)
still edit the *old* library; rerun the retarget tool afterwards.

**Progression / RPG stats (built and wired in):** `scripts/progression/`
+ `resources/progression/progression_table.tres`. Levels 1-20: stat points per
level, health / energy per spent point, energy-mode meter fill, each ability's
power. Classes (Warrior, Gunner, Sneak, Memer) are starting points, not locks.
Abilities scale with their own stat; the unlock table sets when others get
them; Strong Summon gives 3 summons at 10 points of memes. Player reads it all:
level-ups, `spend_stat_point()`, weapon bonus on every hit (`melee_attack()`,
gun shots), `unlocked_abilities()` / `can_use_ability()`, "UNLOCKED" messages,
`starting_class` (on the Player node, saved). Enemy damage scaling uses
`expected_max_health()`. Every number is a placeholder (see each row's notes).
Preview: `scenes/tools/progression_preview.tscn`, or
`scripts/tools/print_progression.gd -- gunner 10 agi=16 foc=15`.

**Weapons:** main slot = any melee weapon; secondary = a gun, or a
dual-wieldable melee weapon next to a dual-wieldable main (then dual
wielding: normal combo in action mode, enables `requires_dual_wield`
abilities like Back Stab). Bat is main-only; Switchblade and Machete are
dual-wieldable; Selfie Stick is the Memer's (main only, scales with memes).
Owned spares (weapons and armour) sit in `Player.weapon_bag`; looted guns
go there too (not straight into SECONDARY). Class kits: Sneak starts
dual wielding, Memer with the Selfie Stick. Drops: Floating Monk (Multi
Arm Man) ~35% one of the three; Mincer 2% Switchblade (`LootDrop.weapon`).

**Inventory + character screens:** separate, each on its own button (I = bag,
C = character), both over the in-game HUD with no dimming, game paused while
either is up. Moving pieces between them needs both open. `InventoryMenu`
runs the session for the pair (pause, keys, the held icon); `StatusMenu` is
the character panel. Moving gear is `GearHand` (`scripts/ui/gear_hand.gd`):
click to pick up, click a slot to place (the old piece goes onto the cursor),
click a bag square to put it in that square (a taken square swaps).
Bag positions live in `Player.bag_layout` (saved; loot takes the first empty square).
Esc / Cancel / clicking off the slots undoes one step;
with nothing held Esc closes; closing the last screen undoes a move in
progress. A refused slot flashes red and says why (`GearHand.why_not`, e.g.
the Bat can't be dual wielded). Only 2 quick items (`Player.quick_items`,
`QUICK_SLOTS`) reach the field; a used-up quick slot keeps its item (greyed,
x0) and picking that item up refills it; other loot goes to the bag.

**Loot:** enemies drop a gold sack on death (`Enemy._drop_loot`, tables in
`resources/enemies/*_stats.tres`, Loot group), cash on a chance, at most one
weapon; the player bends down to loot (`LootBend` state); death is a ragdoll
that dither-fades after 10 s. Notifications stack above the loadout window.

**Lock-on camera:** low and behind when the target is far, Elden Ring style
when close (rises, looks down, over the shoulder on the target's side, side
sticks until the target crosses over). Tuning: Lock-on group in
`player_stats.tres`; code `Player._update_lock_on_camera`.

**Test scene (`scenes/test_scene.tscn`):** player starts as Sneak (dual wield),
pistol sack by spawn, invisible `Bounds` walls at the floor edges, and a
`WaveSpawner` (`scripts/enemy/wave_spawner.gd`, waves are `EnemyWave`
resources on the `Waves` node): a wave from the `Spawn*` markers 30 s after the last one is cleared,
Suit Guys early, Multi Arm Man from wave 4, last wave repeats a level higher.
Wave enemies hunt the player (long sight range, patrol centre on the player).

**Mincer colour:** set by level, not per placement: `strong_tint_from_level`
in `grunt_stats.tres` (5) turns level 5+ Mincers red; below that they keep
their own colours, and their `body_tint` in the levels is ignored.
